How this works

This page never uses the internet. Messages travel only as QR codes on screen, read by a camera — nothing is sent over wifi, data, or any network.

  • To send: type a message, tap the arrow, then let the other device's camera scan the QR code shown fullscreen.
  • To receive: tap "Scan a QR code" and point your camera at the sending device's screen.

Message length: up to about 106 bytes (roughly 100–106 typed characters, fewer if you use emoji or accented letters).

Scanning needs a modern browser (Chrome or Edge on desktop/Android, or Safari 17+). If "Scan" says it's unsupported on this device, you can instead open your phone's built-in Camera or QR scanner app and point it at the sender's QR — that direction still works even without this page's scanner.

If the camera doesn't open when this file is opened directly (double-clicked), some browsers restrict camera access on local files. Try: right-click → open with Chrome, or run a tiny local server from the file's folder (python3 -m http.server) and visit http://localhost:8000 instead.

This same HTML file works identically on your computer and your phone — copy it over by USB, AirDrop, Bluetooth, or email attachment once, and both sides can open it offline from then on.
